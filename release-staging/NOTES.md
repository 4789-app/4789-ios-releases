One beautiful library. One place to press play.

It is an unsigned IPA: sign it with your own Apple Account in SideStore, AltStore Classic, or Sideloadly, then trust the app and enable Developer Mode. ### What to know

- iOS 26 or later. No jailbreak. No 4789 account.
- 4789 supplies software, not media or credentials. Connect services and files you are authorized to use; playable sources come only from what you connect.
- Free signing normally expires after seven days — refresh the app before then, and install updates over the existing app so local data stays in place.
- Watch how it works: [product walkthroughs](https://4789library.com/guides).
